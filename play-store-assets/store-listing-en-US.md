## Full description

Faith Journey Pro is a calm, ad-free Islamic companion designed to help you stay connected to faith throughout the day.

Read and listen to the Holy Quran, explore trusted Hadith collections, find prayer times for your location, face the Qibla with the built-in compass, and keep daily duas and remembrance close at hand.

Key features:

- Read the Quran with Arabic text and translations
- Listen to Quran recitation and continue where you left off
- View location-based prayer times with calculation-method controls
- Find the Qibla direction with an easy-to-use compass
- Browse authentic Hadith collections
- Explore daily duas, morning and evening azkar, and the 99 Names of Allah
- Use the digital Tasbih and track your daily remembrance
- Follow practical guides for Salah, Wudu, fasting, Hajj, Umrah, Zakat, and Janazah
- View the Hijri calendar alongside the Gregorian calendar
- Save bookmarks and sync reading progress when signed in
- Choose your theme, font size, notification preferences, and recitation options

Faith Journey Pro is free to use and contains no advertising. Most reading and learning features work without an account. Location is requested only for features that need it, such as prayer times, the Islamic calendar, and Qibla direction. Notification permissions are optional.

## Data safety working draft

- Data collected: email address and user/account identifiers for authentication; optional profile name/avatar from Google Sign-In; user activity such as bookmarks, reading progress, and streak data when signed in; precise or approximate location when the user invokes location-based features; standard diagnostics/security logs handled by hosting and API providers
- Data shared: coordinates are sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time service only when prayer calculations are requested; authentication and account data are processed by Supabase; Google processes data only when Google Sign-In is chosen
- Data use: app functionality, account management, personalization, security, and reliability
- Security: data encrypted in transit
- Deletion: in-app/web account deletion is available at https://www.faithjourney.pro/account-deletion
